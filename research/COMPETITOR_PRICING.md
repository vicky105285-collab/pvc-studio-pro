## 2. Operator Economics & Value Proposition

- **Cumulative 5-Year Cost of Real PVC Card Pro:** ₹500 × 5 = **₹2,500** + risk of downtime during renewals.
- **Lost Revenue from 1 Day of Software Lockout:** 30 cards × ₹70 = **₹2,100** gross revenue lost in a single day.
- **The PVC Studio Pro Advantage:** 
  1. Permanent offline operational reliability: an e-Sevai centre never loses a working day due to license expiration.
  2. Integrated business ledger and receipt generator replaces separate accounting software or manual registers.
  3. Professional local AI photo background removal eliminates manual Photoshop subscription or external photo studio dependencies.
