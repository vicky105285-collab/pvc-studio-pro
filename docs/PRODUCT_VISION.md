# Product Vision — PVC Studio Pro

**Working Product Name:** PVC Studio Pro  
**Alternative Evaluated Names:** eSevai Card Studio, IDCraft Pro, TamilPVC Pro  
**Recommendation:** **PVC Studio Pro** (Professional, authoritative, easily recognized across all Indian states while retaining high prestige).  
**Target User:** e-Sevai Maiyam operators, CSC VLEs, digital print centres, photo studio owners across India.  

---

## 1. Vision Statement
To empower Indian grassroots digital service centres and cyber cafés with a dependable, precision-engineered Windows desktop production suite that turns citizen digital credentials into physical PVC identity cards with millimeter accuracy, studio-grade AI imaging, and effortless daily business management—100% offline, privacy-compliant, and free from mandatory annual subscription lockouts.

---

## 2. Core Value Tenets
1. **Never Let Down the Shop Operator:** The application must never expire, lockout, or refuse to launch during peak morning business hours. Operational continuity is absolute.
2. **True Physical Precision:** What the operator sees on the preview screen must match the physical printed card on the tray down to 0.1 millimeter.
3. **Studio-Grade Quality with Zero Complexity:** A user with no design or Photoshop knowledge should be able to crop cards, remove photo backgrounds, enhance signatures, and print within 15 seconds.
4. **Absolute Citizen Privacy:** Local-only execution ensures citizen identity data never touches unauthorized external cloud servers.
5. **Cultural & Linguistic Respect:** Equal first-class status for English and Tamil interfaces, reflecting the lived reality of operators in Tamil Nadu.
